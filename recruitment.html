02 — FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I have to be a business major?

No. Alpha Kappa Psi is open to students of every major. Our brothers study everything from finance and marketing to computer science, communications and psychology.

Is AKΨ a social fraternity?

No. We’re a co-ed professional business fraternity focused on career development, leadership and service, though we definitely have fun along the way.

What should I wear?

Each event lists its dress code on the schedule above. Info nights are usually casual; interviews are business professional.

How much time does it take?

Expect weekly chapter meetings plus professional and social events throughout the quarter. We’ll walk through the full commitment at info night.

Are there dues?

Yes, like most organizations there are dues. We’ll share the exact amount and payment options during recruitment.

DM us on Instagram. We’re happy to help.
