01 — About the chapter

Professional growth built through community

Alpha Kappa Psi is a professional business fraternity. At DePaul, the Tau Chi Chapter brings students together to learn, lead, serve, and build relationships that extend beyond college.

Through professional development, chapter events, community engagement, and connection with alumni, members learn from one another and grow as principled leaders.
